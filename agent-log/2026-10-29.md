# Agent log — 2026-10-29 (requested by Monica)

## Summary
**✅ Printed sizes beat estimates.** Monica's test: a plan with sizes written on it still used only the estimate. There were three causes:
1. **Multi-room plans (/plan-rooms):** the AI was told to *ignore* printed dimensions and scale everything from a 1 m door.
2. **Single plans (step 4):** printed sizes only counted when *both* sides came back, already converted by the AI. Centimetres ("375") or a single printed side were thrown away.
3. **Both:** reading small print was a side task inside a big shape prompt.

**The fix:**
- The AI now only *copies* the printed text ("3,75", "375", "3⁷⁵", "12'3\"", "18,4 m²"), and the code works out the units (`_shared/dimensions.ts`).
- The rule: printed sides first. A missing side comes from a printed area, or else from the drawing's proportions. Only when nothing is printed is the estimate used.
- Step 4: copying the printed sizes is now its own focused AI pass, using the stronger model.
- Multi-room plans: rooms without printed sizes are estimated from a scale taken from the rooms that have them, with the 1 m door as the last resort.
- The plan screens say which side is "written on plan", "from printed area" or "estimated".

**✅ Step 6 parts.** All five parts are clickable in the same order on steps 5 and 6: Main furniture, Accents, Tables & chairs, Walls & floors, Decor. Walls & floors (a finish per wall and the floor) and Decor (switch suggestions on or off, add your own) are new. Both feed the buying list, the step 8 walls and the summary.

**⚠️ Deploy:** the plan fix is mostly in the edge functions `analyze-floorplan` and `analyze-multiroom-plan`, plus the new `_shared/dimensions.ts`. Lovable has to redeploy them; until then the app can only use what the old functions return.

**🧪 Results:** tests 61/61 (14 new). Type check and build pass. Lint is at 161 errors, unchanged. QA: 37/39 screens. 1.6 and 6 differ from the old designs on purpose (shared step strip; step 6 split into parts), and step 6's missing words are now marked optional.
